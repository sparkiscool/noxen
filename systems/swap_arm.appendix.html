Assembly order

  1. Stub side: press the two 6010-2RS into the hub's sleeve from the stub end, slide the hub on the stub, screw the post on; axle stepper and 16T pulley on the stub's ear, belt round the hub's ring, detent arm with its 623ZZ on its pivot.
  2. Each V rail on the hub's plate: two M5 x 10 into the heat-set M5 inserts.
  3. Each carriage: four M3 inserts in the spine, the MGN12 rail on it with four M3 x 12 (block on the rail, stoppers off only at the bench); three V wheels, two 625ZZ in each, on M5 x 20 axles, the third on its eccentric bush; roll it onto the V rail and set the bush until it runs without play. The tab on the spine's side is the pick-up stop: the lift's nut lug meets it as the block's top reaches the rail's end.
  4. Each screw drive: stepper on its face plate (four M3), coupler, inner F8-16M, screw through the thrust wall, outer F8-16M, lock ring; the nut on the screw with its flange's two sides filed to the body.
  5. Each lift: bond the three o 6 x 115 carbon rods in the yoke's sockets (resin through each port until it shows at the mouth), slide the two ties on and bond them at their stations (float bolt centres 45 hub side and 20 far side of the tab line); four M4 inserts in each pair of ties.
  6. Yoke on the MGN block with four M3 x 12; the nut into its lug (flange in the slot); the return spring from the carriage to the rail's channel.
  7. Each grab unit (the steps and their sweeps: parts/box_arm8/kit.json test_set): servo in from the open side, two M3 x 8 through its inner ear; paddle onto the spline, M3 x 35 from the hub end; each pilot on two cut o 3 springs, its o 3 x 9 pin in from the blade bay; blades and their four springs, the two o 3 x 66 rods through; an SS49E in each hall post; the eight TPU pads into their seats.
  8. Grab unit on the ties: four M4 shoulder bolts down through the base with a PTFE and a steel washer under each head, a float spring round each bolt between tie and base.
  9. Ring: each bay's receiver on its saddle by the receiver's own mount bolts, the 20 link bars between neighbouring ears.
  10. Set up: with a cartridge gripped, each pad must touch the pack's bottom: swap a pad for the next thickness where it does not, then glue; park by the screw's position; check both hall readings with the bolts in and out.
