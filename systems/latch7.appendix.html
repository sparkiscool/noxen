Open

1. The Scout's plug is 42.8 mm from the pull and its flat bottom ends 21.9 mm the other way, so the grip carries x2.88 the plug's force. v7 is sized for it. The cause stays: only moving the tab line toward the plug or a lower-force connector removes it.

2. The spring's rate and closed length are not on the listing: weigh one (1.0 to 1.4 N at 21 mm) and close one fully (9.0 mm or less).

3. Friction, the XT60's 57 N and the blade springs' 15 N are estimates or single readings.
