The funnel was drawn round estimated outlines. Against the real latch sets its hole (170 x 50) was too small for the Overseer cartridge (174 x 55) and its seat (66.8 wide) too narrow for the Overseer receiver (68.0). Now: hole 177 x 57, seat 68.8 wide. The whole Scout v4 and Overseer still clear it: 0 crossings seated and in all 65 landing states each (tools/seat_check.py).

The Scout's receiver is a 51 mm wide keel with ends clipped at 45 degrees: it hung in the hole and nothing held it along its length (over 6 mm of play each way). It now has four landing feet, one on a leg down from each corner boss: pads 4.8 x 6.0 mm on the funnel's side ledges, their outer ends 0.3 mm from the end walls' feet, the same 68 mm across as the Overseer's. 24.9 g in all, part of the receiver print. At 16.0 N a foot (the XT60 pull-out on four feet, or a 3 g landing on two) a leg's root is at index 0.25. They clear the Scout v4's own parts, the cartridge's path and the grabber (the receiver's TPU edge skids are gone, 14 Nov: v4 stands on its own feet). Both tab lines sit at the same place along the arm's axis (0.2 mm apart).
