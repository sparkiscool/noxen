# Desktop dual-board case, upright suitcase (25 Nov)

Stands on its end the whole time it runs. Closed 372 x 126 x 299 mm, plus the 22 mm display door on the front, 11 mm feet and a 55 mm handle on top.

Layout, bottom up: foot rails with TPU pads (the left rail reaches 150 mm out as an outrigger for the open door) | fan base (your Kootek pad's 120 mm and three 70 mm fans stand flat and blow up) | bay with two boards standing upright side by side, hot sides facing a 60 mm chimney | lid with slots and the fifth 70 mm fan as an extractor | handle on top.

Air: in through the base's rear and end slots and the floor bores, up the chimney, out the lid and the upper end walls. Front and bay rear are solid.

Door: the 15.6 in panel and driver board, hinged on the left vertical edge, opens to about 175 degrees beside the case, two latches on the right. No fold-out legs.

Box is fitted: width = the panel's 360 + 6, depth = the 120 mm fan, height = board 220 + 8 standoff + 17 under the lid. Board sizes are estimates (outlines unpublished), so mounts wait for measuring.

63 flat 3 mm PCTG tiles plus the handle, rails and TPU pads (about 3.3 kg), none over 180 mm, no supports. Hand calcs in make.py's docstring. Not yet through the assembly check, nothing sent to the printers.
