# Desktop dual-board case, redesign around the Kootek pad (24 Nov)

**Layout:** 780 x 310 x 96 mm, two bays side by side (each 385 x 304 x 90 inside), solid divider. 42 flat 3 mm panels, none over 180 mm.

**Cooling = your Kootek pad** (Amazon B01469DJLM, ordered 24 Nov; listing 380 x 300 x 35 mm, one 120 mm + four 70 mm fans, USB powered; no airflow figure published). Each bay is a pad seat: the pad lies on the floor, the board sits on it like a laptop on a pad, and the pad's fans blow up into the cooler's bottom intake. The pad draws its air through the slotted floor. Hot air leaves through the slotted rear wall (laptop coolers exhaust rearward) and the slotted lid. No extra case fans. It suits: it fits a bay with 5 mm to spare; you have one pad, so bay B is an empty second seat until you buy another.

**Power:** one 14 mm port low in the rear wall at each outer end, one per board's brick.

**Boards:** TIMI A7S TM2037 chassis size, board outline, holes and cooler layout are not published anywhere found. Board stack (about 360 x 250 x 30) is an estimate for a 15.6 in gaming laptop. Interior mounts wait for measuring and the second board's name.

Print: panels flat, PCTG, no supports; `print/desktop_dual_board_case_<panel>.stl`. Nothing sent to the printers.
